# Docs Assist 1.0 release plan

1.0 is one comprehensive overhaul, not a series of increments.

Edward's call, 2026-09-16: no other work merges before it, all open pull requests close, and anything worth keeping from them gets absorbed here.
A comprehensive refactor is acceptable.

The product design this implements is [The ideal documentation plugin](../reports/ideal-plugin-design-2026-09-16.md).
This file is the build order.

The previous version of this file staged a path from 0.9.8 through 0.9.11 to 1.0.0.
That plan is superseded.

## What 1.0 is

A documentation plugin organized around a **three-chair authoring loop**.

1. An **authority chair** emits a cold, prose-free packet of steps, code, citations, and flat statements.
1. A **continuity chair** reconciles that packet against the rest of the documentation set, so examples cohere and share values.
1. An **advocate chair** shapes it into a document and declares, as a ledger, everything it added that the packet did not contain.

The chairs are cast per document along two axes: where the truth lives, and what the reader needs.
Passes repeat with a descending tolerance threshold until convergence, a pass cap, or an oscillation that escalates to the human.

The loop's real output is not the polished document.
It is a short list of what genuinely needs a human.

## Salvage inventory

Nothing worth keeping is lost by closing the outstanding work.

### Absorbed from PR #13

1. **`external-verification.md`**, the specific-plus-generic search heuristic and the self-citation exclusion. The authority chair needs both, and the exclusion becomes always-on rather than opt-in.
1. **The `llms.txt` altitude test** and its inline-versus-pointer rule. Moves to the preparation gate.

Reframed rather than carried: section-level verification markers become a suggested trust and retrieval improvement, offered with the benefit stated and added only on a yes.

### Absorbed from the field report

1. **Bundle drift checks** become a parameterized, stack-agnostic script (Phase 1).
1. **Auditor web reach** becomes a tool grant on the authority chair, since external claims are most of what it verifies.
1. **The finish gate** becomes the preparation gate command.
1. **The attested-claims fallback** is subsumed entirely. The loop's own `questions.md` is the durable ledger that finding was asking for, so no separate convention is needed.

### Absorbed from Track 1

1. **Reader-language coverage** becomes an input to the advocate chair, fed by the harvester.
1. **Negative results** become rows in the packet schemas: known misconceptions for concepts, hard limits for procedures.
1. **Human-first framing** becomes the doctrine for how trust signals are offered.

### Surviving from the current plugin, unchanged in spirit

Dump-first intake, now populating the packet.
Execution verification, now paired with the continuity chair.
Guide-never-gate.
Solo-with-team-rigor as the cold default.
The reference registry, now with an owner.
`doc-recon` and `doc-intake`, now feeding packets.

### Cut

`doc-drafter` and `doc-auditor`, replaced by the chairs.
The standalone second-opinion pass, subsumed by the loop.
Frontmatter as plugin state.
Automatic close-out linting.
Most of the command surface.

## Build order

Dependencies drive the order.

An eval harness was originally scheduled ahead of the loop, on the argument that the loop's quality cannot be assessed by reading it.
That was reversed on 2026-09-16: an eval built before the implementation exists is designed against an imagined implementation, which is the same over-fitting risk in a different costume.
Build the thing, then test against it and iterate.
The two dry runs stand as the seed corpus when that starts, including their two false positives, which are the more valuable cases.

### Phase 1: Deterministic foundation and state relocation

**Complete, 2026-09-16.** No design dependencies. Worth having regardless.

1. `assets/ci/example-continuity.mjs`: extract every literal from every code block across the docs set, cluster by role (hostname, port, token, username, path, resource name), and flag any cluster holding more than one value.
1. `assets/ci/bundle-drift.mjs`: forward drift, reverse drift, marker parity, and element parity, with the marker and element vocabulary declared per project rather than assuming one site generator's syntax.
1. `assets/ci/duration-check.mjs`: a document's stated duration against the sum of its step durations. Every quickstart promising five minutes is a candidate.
1. `assets/ci/file-path-check.mjs`: a code block whose content is file contents rather than commands, with no path declared on the block and none in the surrounding prose. Naming the file a reader must edit is the cheapest documentation failure to prevent and one of the most common.

A language-tag check was proposed here and cut.
"The tag does not match the content" fired a false positive on the first real document it was tried against, where `java` was a deliberate accommodation for `sshd_config` syntax the highlighter has no grammar for.
Only the unambiguous forms are safe: no tag at all, or a tag the configured highlighter does not recognize.
This is the same lesson `leverage` and `just` already taught the Vale styles.

Finally, move all plugin state to `.docs-assist/`.
Frontmatter becomes something the plugin offers to wire up when a site generator is present, and nothing the plugin depends on.

Keep `check-claims.mjs`, `check-facts.mjs`, `docs-decay.mjs`, and `docs-impact.mjs`.

### Phase 2: Constitutions

**Complete, 2026-09-16.** Reference files under the single skill, loaded by path.

```text
skills/docs-assist/reference/
  loop.md                    chairs, artifacts, run order, termination, cost
  casting.md                 the two axes, the cast matrix, the two-casts test
  packet-procedure.md        prerequisites, steps, provenance, limits, unknowns
  packet-concept.md          model, causal chains, misconceptions, boundaries
  ledger.md                  entry types and which route to the authority chair
  style-stack.md             precedence, the Google and GitLab conflicts, recording resolutions
  personas.md                the project overlay between constitution and runtime brief
  external-verification.md   claims about what the project does not vendor
  chairs/
    shared-rules.md          loaded by every chair, every pass
    authority/{contract,pass-1-correctness,pass-2-assumptions,pass-3-misleading}.md
    advocate/{contract,pass-1-shape,pass-2-clarity,pass-3-precision}.md
    continuity/contract.md
```

A chair loads `shared-rules.md`, its own contract, and the one file for its current pass.
The escalation gradient is implemented as which rulebook is in context, not as an instruction to be stricter.

`shared-rules.md` exists so the no-quota principle and the check-before-you-claim rules are single-sourced and still in context for every chair on every pass.

`external-verification.md` is the salvage from PR #13, with the self-citation exclusion promoted from an opt-in mode to always-on.

`second-opinion.md` is reframed rather than deleted: it is the loop with one pass, one chair, and no packet, and it stays valid for the workflows that have not moved to the loop and for work too short to pay for a full run.

### Phase 3: Chairs and orchestrator

**Complete, 2026-09-16**, except `doc-harvester.md`, which moved to Phase 4 where it belongs with persona building and where the consent question is settled.

1. `agents/chair-authority.md`, `chair-advocate.md`, `chair-continuity.md`, `cold-reader.md`. Thin wrappers: name, routing description, tool grant, and a pointer to the constitution.
1. Tool grants carry the structural half of the forbidden moves. Reviewing chairs get no `Write`.
1. Orchestrator doctrine in `SKILL.md`: casting, pass management, path passing, and the after-pass check that only the expected artifact changed.
1. Artifact layout under `.docs-assist/loop/<doc-slug>/`.
1. Retire `doc-drafter` and `doc-auditor`.

### Phase 4: Surface reduction

**Complete, 2026-09-16.** Moved ahead of the persona layer: an audit after Phase 3 found the old surface actively contradicting the new machinery, and those contradictions compound with every phase added on top.

1. **Intake and the packet are one artifact.** The intake loop is pass 0 with a person in the authority chair. `.docs-assist/intake/notes/` is gone; everything lands in `.docs-assist/loop/<doc-slug>/packet.md`. A packet a human filled and one `chair-authority` emitted are interchangeable, so a doc can start conversationally and finish with the loop or the reverse.
1. **An audit is the loop pointed at a finished doc.** There is no separate audit mechanism. The difference is only that pass 0 *reconstructs* the packet from the existing document instead of writing one, and a claim nobody can source is the finding.
1. **Commands: 14 down to 9.** The design said roughly four, and that was too aggressive. The right cut is doors against plumbing, not a number. Doors kept: health, draft, plan, audit, verify, update, release-notes. Plumbing folded: `init`, `setup-lint`, `setup-hooks`, and `setup-site` merged into one `setup`; `make-examples` and `template` into drafting; `agent-ready` into the new `merge-prep`.
1. **The preparation gate exists**: `commands/merge-prep.md`. Whole-set continuity, `llms.txt` generation, bundle drift, deterministic checks, linters, link check. Human-invoked only.
1. **The Phase 1 scripts are wired.** They were built and then referenced almost nowhere; `bundle-drift.mjs` was dead code. Now offered through `setup` as CI checks and run by `health` and `audit`.
1. **Pass 0 has a rulebook.** The escalation table started at pass 1, leaving the advocate chair shaping with no rules assigned. Shape is the only thing decided twice, because it is the only thing the first round of feedback can invalidate.
1. Trust signals offered with their benefit stated, never applied unasked.

### Phase 5: Persona layer

**Complete, 2026-09-16.**

1. `agents/doc-harvester.md` mines issue replies, pull request review comments, and commit message bodies. It returns an inventory and never raw content, and it reads commit *bodies* rather than subjects, because a subject says what changed and a body sometimes says why.
1. `reference/harvest.md` carries the method and the privacy rules, which are stricter than the plugin's defaults because the source is: opt-in per project, summarize never paste, public sources only unless told otherwise, no customer identifiers, security content excluded entirely, and the raw corpus dropped when done.
1. Persona generation is a stage in `/docs-assist:setup`, building `.docs-assist/personas/authority.md` and `advocate.md`. Harvesting is a **separate yes** inside that stage: vocabulary and reader baseline alone make a usable persona, so a declined harvest degrades the overlay rather than blocking it.
1. Three-layer resolution per `personas.md`: constitution, then overlay, then runtime brief.

One corpus, three uses, which is why it is one capability rather than three: propose a draft from what was already explained, build the authority persona's voice, and supply real reader language. That last one is the evidence `ia-methodology.md` has always asked for and never had a method for gathering.

The draft proposal is the activation-energy fix and the largest single lever here. "You explained this in three issues last month, here is a draft, is it right?" beats a blank page, and it inverts who does the work.

**Not run against any repository.** The capability ships with its gate; pointing it at a tracker is the contributor's call.

### Phase 6: Test, iterate, and ship

1. Run the whole thing end to end on a repository that is not this one.
1. Rewrite `README.md`, `docs/command-reference.md`, and `docs/plan.md` against what 1.0 actually is.
1. Write the 1.0.0 CHANGELOG entry.
1. Bump to 1.0.0, by hand.

## Definition of done

1. The loop catches every bug in the Phase 2 corpus.
1. An audit of a documentation set about something the project does not vendor produces at least one finding its maintainer did not already know.
1. A reader can work straight through a multi-document journey without an example breaking or a value changing under them.
1. No plugin behavior depends on frontmatter.
1. Nothing expensive runs without being asked for.
1. The whole flow has been dogfooded on a repository other than this one.
1. `scripts/validate.mjs` and the configured linters pass clean.

## Open items

1. **Branch cleanup.** Twelve local branches carry no unique work, and most exist on `origin` too. Deleting shared refs needs an explicit go-ahead, separate from closing the pull requests.
1. **Harvest consent.** Building the authority persona means reading your issue replies, pull request comments, and commit bodies. Your own public activity in your own repository, but the doctrine keeps anything touching a ticket tracker opt-in.
1. **A second dogfooding target.** `edwardangert.github.io` is the only real consumer on record, and it is one Astro and Starlight site documenting one subject. Every finding in this plan is over-fitted to it until something else exercises the loop.

## Risks

1. **Scope.** This is a rewrite of the plugin's center while keeping its doctrine. The mitigation is Phase 1 and Phase 2 shipping value before any chair exists, so the effort is not all-or-nothing.
1. **Cost.** Up to nine subagent runs per document. Deterministic checks first, convergence, a single-pass mode, and fan-out across documents.
1. **Two agents agreeing confidently and both being wrong.** Neither chair holds ground truth. The authority chair must cite repository evidence for any correction it applies, and an uncited assertion becomes a human question rather than an edit.
1. **Continuity flattening real differences.** Any substitution that could change behavior goes back as a question, never applied as an edit.
1. **Over-fitting to one site.** Every item passes the portability filter before it ships: general, project-specific, or split.
