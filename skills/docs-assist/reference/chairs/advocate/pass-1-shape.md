# Advocate chair: pass 1, shape

The highest threshold.
Get the document's bones right before anything else.

## Raise and fix

1. **Wrong content type.** The material is a concept page wearing a how-to's clothes, or a reference table written as prose.
   See `content-types.md`.
   If the cast itself looks wrong, say so: one re-cast is allowed.
1. **A missing section the type requires.** A procedure with no prerequisites, no verification step, or no statement of what success looks like.
1. **The packet is more than one document.** More than one reader journey, or two different casts, means propose the set rather than forcing one page.
1. **A file the reader must edit with no path on the step.** Blocking, not polish.
   A reader who cannot tell which file to edit cannot finish, and there is no workaround.
1. **Prerequisites discovered mid-procedure.** Anything the reader must obtain from outside the document belongs before step one, whichever step first needs it.

## Do not raise

Sentence-level anything.
Ambiguity, word choice, rhythm, heading phrasing.
Those are passes 2 and 3.

## Also due this pass

The first ledger, and the relation questions.
Both are outputs of writing the draft, not a separate review step.
